# Scoring Rubric — used at FOCUS

Score each band that survived PUNCH HOLES on four dimensions, 1–5 each. Total out of 20. Use the totals to rank, then surface the top 2–3 for the human to pick from at FOCUS.

## Dimensions

### Differentiation (1–5)
How far is this from the red-ocean takes mapped in step 1?
- 1 = a rephrase of a dominant narrative
- 5 = genuinely absent from current coverage; the reader has not seen this

### Audience relevance (1–5)
How much does *this specific audience* care?
- 1 = clever but tangential to what they actually worry about
- 5 = hits a problem they feel acutely

### Defensibility (1–5)
Can you actually back this with evidence — stats, sources, precedent?
- 1 = a vibe; you would be asserting it
- 5 = strong data and credible sources already exist
*An undefensible angle is a liability, not an asset. Weight this honestly.*

### On-brand fit (1–5)
Does this fit the creator's or brand's voice, values, and positioning?
- 1 = off-key or off-strategy for whoever is publishing it
- 5 = sounds like them at their sharpest

## Ranking
Sum to a score out of 20. Rank highest to lowest. Surface the top 2–3 to the human with scores and a one-line case each.

## Tie-breakers and judgment
- Prefer a slightly-less-differentiated angle that is highly defensible over a wildly original one you cannot source.
- Flag any angle scoring 5 on differentiation but ≤2 on defensibility as **high-risk / high-reward**, so the human chooses with eyes open.
- Do not average away a 1. An angle with a 1 in any dimension usually has a fatal flaw worth naming out loud rather than burying in an average.
